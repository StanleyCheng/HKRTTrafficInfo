# Hong Kong Road Traffic Information

This context names the geographic records and map controls used to explore Hong Kong traffic information.

## Language

**Map item**:
A selectable geographic record or road segment, including cameras, detectors, incidents, parking, rainfall, crossings, works, tolls, boundary points, stops, stations, piers, and moving transport records.

**Sailing corridor**:
The smooth water path drawn between two piers. Route lines, timetable estimates and projected GPS movement all follow the same corridor in either direction.

**Item popup**:
The information shown next to a selected map item.

**Map layer**:
A selectable group of related map items.

**Layer category**:
A named group of related map layers presented together for browsing. Choosing a category preserves which map layers are visible.

**Sub-layer control**:
An independent visibility control for a map layer within a category. Several sub-layers and routes may be visible across categories at the same time.

**Global controls**:
Search, layer management, and feed or refresh information that apply to the map rather than to a single item.
