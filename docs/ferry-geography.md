# Ferry map geometry

The map draws one inferred water route for each of the 16 supported pier pairs. Both directions use the same geometry in reverse. Route lines remain visible between sailings; timetable estimates and movement projected from Sun Ferry fixes use those same lines.

These lines are illustrative sailing corridors, not surveyed vessel tracks or navigation advice. The Transport Department's [ferry route GeoJSON](https://static.data.gov.hk/td/routes-fares-geojson/JSON_FERRY.json) provides pier **points**, not sailing polylines. Pier positions come from that dataset, with separate North Point west (Sun Ferry) and east (Fortune Ferry) berths. [Sun Ferry's Central–Mui Wo route page](https://www.sunferry.com.hk/en/route-and-fare?origin=CE&destination=MW) identifies Central Pier 6; Cheung Chau uses Pier 5.

The bends were inferred against the Lands Department's [iB50000 map data](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/metadata/landsd_rcd_1637221775627_85634/html), retrieved on 10 October 2026. `HYDRLINE` coastline (`COA`) supports visual inspection; the regression fixture uses the [HYDRPOLY Sea Fill export](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/server/rest/services/common/landsd_rcd_1637221775627_85634/MapServer/7/query?where=TYPE%3D%27SEF%27&outFields=CLASS,TYPE,GRAPHICID,OBJECTID&outSR=4326&returnGeometry=true&f=geojson) (`SEF`, defined in the [official data dictionary](https://www.landsd.gov.hk/landsd_psi_data/SMO/data/iB50000_Data_Dictionary-GMLv.1.1.pdf)). These are water polygons with holes for islands and shore structures. `LANDPOLY` alone does not provide a complete harbour land mask.

The fixture retains unsimplified polygons covering all supported crossings, with coordinates rounded to seven decimal places. Tests sample every segment at intervals of at most five metres in both directions, then split each segment at every coastline intersection and check the intervening intervals. This catches rocks and deck edges narrower than the sampling distance. They also check that clock and projected GPS movement follow the displayed geometry. Only the first and last 35 metres are exempt because official stop points can lie on pier decks. The routes were also independently sampled against the original Sea Fill polygons. This verifies the mapped water corridors against the source resolution; it does not establish navigational clearance or exact operator sailing tracks.

The fixture acknowledges Government of Hong Kong SAR and Lands Department intellectual property, with CSDI Portal attribution and [source terms](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/doc/TNC).

`lib/ferry-fairway.ts` owns all route geometry. `ferryPaths()` creates the persistent overlays, while `ferryFairway()` supplies boat paths. Leaflet simplification is disabled for these lines so zooming out cannot discard a bend around an island. Replace the inferred corridors with operator-provided sailing polylines if those become available.
